# Should the three tips open by themselves on a first visit, or wait until the reader asks?

*Status: decided. The owner chose "wait until asked" on 25 September 2026.*

## A few words, defined once

- **The tips** are a short strip of three cards: touch a verse, press and drag, touch a chip.
  They teach the three things you can do on the page.
- **Settings** is the sheet that opens from the app's name at the top. It already holds the
  language choice and the page-bar magnifier switch.

## Why was this asked now?

The owner opened the app and said the tutorial should not happen by default; it should be a
button in settings. Up to then the strip came up on a device's first visit and never again
once dismissed. Each time the app was shown to someone new, the tips covered the first thing
they saw.

## What happens if nobody decides?

Every first visit, including every demo, opens with a teaching strip taking space above the
page.

## What were the options?

| option | pros | cons | what it commits us to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Today**: the strip opens on the first visit, then never again | a new reader is taught without looking for it | it gets in front of the page in every demo; a reader who skipped it cannot get it back | nothing new |
| **Wait until asked**: a "Show the tips" button in settings | the app opens straight onto the page; the tips can be shown again as often as wanted | a new reader has to find the button | settings has to stay easy to find |

## So what was decided?

**Wait until asked.** The strip no longer opens by itself. A "Show the tips" button in the
settings sheet closes the sheet and opens the strip from its first card, every time it is
pressed. Skip, Escape and Done still close it. The storage warning, which shares the strip's
place above the page, now shows first and steps aside while the tips are open.

## What is this not settling?

- What the tips say. The three cards are unchanged.
- Whether a first-time reader should get a small hint pointing at settings. Nobody has asked
  for one.
