How should The Study Quran's note sit on a phone?

On an iPad the note sits beside the verse it is about. A phone has no room beside, so the note rises as a card from the foot of the screen. Measured on the longest verse on the demo's path, that card showed the note's title and the translation, and not one line of the book's commentary, whether the phone was upright or on its side. Each way of fixing it was built into the app. The first of each pair is the default; the others are a setting in the info panel.

What it changes for a hafiz: tapping a verse on a phone shows the book's commentary on it at once, with the verse still whole in sight, instead of a translation that has to be scrolled past first.

The note
The Study Quran's translation of a verse and its commentary on it, opened by tapping the verse.
The commentary
the book's explanation of the verse, under the translation.
Upright, on its side
the phone held tall (portrait) or wide (landscape).

In the drawings the orange bands are the verse's lines on the page, the grey bars are the translation's words, and the green bars are the commentary's. No words are drawn.

What should the note lead with, held upright?

A · Two lines of the translation, then the commentarythe default

The translation is cut to two lines with a button for the rest; the commentary starts inside the short note.

B · The whole translation first, as it was

The note shows the whole translation, and the commentary starts below it.

C · The note opens grown to the full height

The note rises over the whole screen at once, showing the translation and much of the commentary.

What it buysWhat it costsWhat it commits us to
A · Two lines of the translation, then the commentary
  • The reader sees the book's own words on the verse at once, without a scroll.
  • The verse stays whole and clear above the note.
  • The whole translation is one tap away, and the tap is undone the same way.
  • The translation is cut mid-sentence until the reader asks for it.
  • One more button in the note.
The note leads with the commentary on a phone, while the iPad keeps leading with the whole translation.
B · The whole translation first, as it was
  • Nothing is cut: the translation reads whole.
  • The same order as on the iPad.
  • On a long verse the commentary is out of sight until the reader scrolls, so a first look shows no commentary at all.
A phone reader has to learn to scroll the note before the book's commentary appears.
C · The note opens grown to the full height
  • The most commentary in sight at once.
  • Nothing is cut.
  • The verse it is about is hidden under it, so the reader loses the page while reading about it.
  • Closing the note is needed to see the page again.
A phone reads the note as a page of its own, not beside the verse.

Where should the note stand with the phone on its side?

A · Standing beside the page, the full height of the screenthe default

Held on its side, the note stands at the side of the screen and the page moves over to make room.

B · Lying across the foot of the screen, as it was

The note lies across the bottom of the screen, under the page.

What it buysWhat it costsWhat it commits us to
A · Standing beside the page, the full height of the screen
  • The verse and the commentary are both in sight, side by side, as on the iPad.
  • The note is as tall as the screen allows, which on its side is the scarce direction.
  • It covers the right-hand end of the page strip and the chip at the foot while it is open.
  • On the smallest phones (375 points tall) the bars at the foot still cover the end of a long verse.
A phone on its side behaves like a small iPad: the note beside the page, not over it.
B · Lying across the foot of the screen, as it was
  • The same place as when the phone is upright.
  • The whole page strip at the foot is covered alike, so nothing is half hidden.
  • No commentary in sight until the reader scrolls the strip.
  • The strip is too short to read in.
A phone reader turns the phone upright to read a note.

Who decides, and what happens if nobody does?

The owner, by trying each in the pitch build on a phone (the info panel: "What a note on a phone shows first" and "Where a note stands on a phone on its side"). Until then the app shows the first of each, and nothing else changes: the iPad and the computer are not touched by either choice.
