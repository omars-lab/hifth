# Which parts of the app could be paid for inside the app?

*Started 2026-11-08 from the owner's words: put paid features behind an in-app purchase, a lot
of the sharing for a start; none of the Qur'an itself is gated; maybe a purchase for a specific
text. Nothing is built. This page holds the questions until they are answered.*

## The short version

**The rule that is already settled:** the Qur'an is never behind a payment. Reading it, turning
its pages, finding a verse and listening to it stay free for everyone, always (owner,
2026-11-08).

**What could be paid for:** the extras around the reading. The owner named sharing first (sending
a verse, a page or a revision record to someone). A specific text, such as a commentary, could
be its own purchase.

**What stands in the way:** Apple sells in-app purchases only through an App Store release.
TestFlight testers never pay. An App Store release is held today by the open question about the
verse-link data's licence and the App Store (backlog: app-store-not-a-target). So paid features
come after that question, not before it.

## What does it change for a hafiz?

Nothing in their revision: the page, the marks, the look-alike verses and the recitation stay
free. A hafiz who wants to send their revision to a teacher, or read a scholar's commentary
beside the verse, might pay for that.

## Open questions, and what would answer each

### ① Which features are paid, and which stay free? · **open**

The agent drafts a list of every feature, with a suggested free or paid next to each and the
reason, starting from the owner's "a lot of the sharing". The Qur'an, its pages, the search and
the recitation are on the free side by rule. The owner picks.

### ② Could a specific text, such as a commentary, be sold inside the app? · **open**

The owner's idea of a purchase for a specific text. It turns on who owns the text and what they
agree to. For The Study Quran, that is part of the collaboration we are pitching, not something
we could decide alone.

### ③ Can the app be sold on the App Store at all, given the verse-link data's licence? · **blocked**

In-app purchases need an App Store release. The same licence question already holds the App
Store (backlog: app-store-not-a-target); the licence brief for TestFlight should ask this one
too, so the reader answers both at once.

### ④ Build the purchase and the switch that unlocks a paid feature · **blocked**

Apple's purchase system in the iPad and Mac app, and one switch the web app reads to show or
hide each paid feature, so the free path is tested on its own. Waits on the list (①) and on the
App Store question (③).
