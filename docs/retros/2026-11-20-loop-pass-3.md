# Loop pass 3: larger text on a real iPhone screen

**What was done.** The rough build of larger text was opened on the iPhone SE simulator at its
largest text size, in English and Arabic. The boxes grew but the words did not. A small test page
showed why: the app's "keep text at 100%" rule also holds inside a grown part on iPhone Safari.
One line in the grown parts fixed it (`docs/issues/text-grow-words-did-not-grow.md`). With the
words really growing, two faults showed up: the info button is pushed off the top bar, and the
verse drawer covers the chosen verse. Both come before the owner is asked to choose a way.

A helper added axe scans for the remaining screens. All are clean except the madd colour mark in
the mistake picker, at about 3.9 to 1 against the 4.5 asked. A darker madd colour would change it
everywhere, so that is the owner's call. A second helper folded the two Nov 13 session notes into
five skills, and last pass's two skill lines landed too.

**What went wrong, and what to keep.**

- The test browsers grew the words either way, so the browser test passed before the fix and
  after it. Only the simulator showed the fault. The walk checklist now has the step.
- The app's offline copy kept serving the old build on the same port, with a "new version"
  banner over the first pictures. A fresh port for each build got round it.
- The helpers' new working copies were refused: the limit is 5 and there were already 7. They
  reused two idle ones. In a reused copy, a helper has to run `intent start` and `pnpm install`
  first, or its commit misses its task label (the folding commit has none).
- The three drawn backlog pages clashed only on their stamp lines when the helpers' work came
  in; the data file itself merged cleanly. Redrawing after the merge settles the stamps.

**For a skill.** `walk-app`: say near the top that a new build gets a fresh port in the
simulator, not only in the checklist line. `extend` (or wherever helpers are briefed): a helper
in a reused working copy runs `intent start <row>` and `pnpm install` before its first commit.
`issues`: when only the stamp lines of the drawn pages clash, take either side and redraw.
