## Snags
- Readers working from page pictures shrunk to half size missed a slanted word now and then
  (18:52, 18:62). A full-size pass over the doubtful calls caught both. Reading at full size
  should be the default for any by-eye slant batch.
- Counting slanted runs per verse made the hand reading look like it lost slant (net -14). Counted
  by words, it gained 165 and dropped 18: the hand rows join neighbouring words into one run.
  Compare words, never runs.

## Worth copying
- Before merging hand-read rows that switch off an automatic rule, list every word the rule
  slanted that the rows leave upright, and check each one on the print. 17 of 18 were the rule's
  mistakes, and 1 was a real miss. That gives a measured miss rate, not a guess.
- Copy the built notes before re-extracting, then compare verse by verse. Only the read verses,
  and verses sharing a paragraph with them, may change, and no note's words may change.
