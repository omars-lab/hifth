## Snags
- The accessibility row said only the madd mark was faint. Measuring all seven by hand found
  four short; axe skips letters it judges to be decoration, so it saw only one. A passing axe
  scan after darkening madd alone would have hidden the other three. When axe flags one item of
  a set, measure the whole set.
- A picture script outside `apps/web` cannot `import "playwright"`; NODE_PATH does not reach ES
  imports. `createRequire(<apps/web/package.json>)("@playwright/test")` does.
- Switching tajweed colouring on and taking a strip of the page showed no visible difference at
  that size, so the page pictures were dropped rather than shipped as filler.

## Worth copying
- An options page's pictures can be made with no app change at all: open the real built app,
  lay each option's few lines of style over it, and screenshot one element. Cheap to redo.
- To find a darker shade that passes, keep the hue and saturation and step lightness down until
  the contrast reaches 4.6 to 1; the result still reads as the same colour.
