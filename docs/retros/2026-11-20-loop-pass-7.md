## Snags
- The commit hook refused the last pass's commit on `gate:tasks` (the backlog pages drawn from
  an older source) because only half the change was staged: the drawn pages compare against the
  registers on disk. Stage a task's registers and its drawn pages in one commit, and run
  `make -s issues-doc tasks-doc` last, after the final register edit.
- Comparing Arabic words for a keyword check needs the vowel marks dropped first
  (`normalize("NFD")`, then remove `\p{M}`): كَلِمة and كلمة are one word to a reader, two to a
  plain string compare.
