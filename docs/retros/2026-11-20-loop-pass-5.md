# Loop pass 5: the iPhone has less room than the test browsers

**What was done.** The fixed larger-text build was walked on the iPhone SE simulator at its
largest text size. The top bar fitted. The chosen verse was still covered, because Safari's own
bars leave the page about 500 px of height, not 667, and Safari shows the "Install Hifth" banner,
which the test browsers never show. A browser test at 375 by 500 with the banner went red first.
In the larger-text build, the banner now waits on a phone while a verse's drawer is open, and the
drawer grows only as far as the room under the verse allows. The simulator then showed the whole
verse, in English at 1.5 times and in Arabic following the phone.

The Nov 13 front-page merge note was folded into `testing`.

**What went wrong, and what to keep.**

- The first fix only fitted the drawer. The test passed, but the simulator showed the drawer had
  given up growing and still half-covered the second line. A passing test about room is not
  enough; add a check that the thing still does its job (here, that the drawer still grows).
- `make text-grow-e2e` ends by rebuilding the ordinary app, so a preview server started after it
  serves the ordinary app. Use `make text-grow-serve` for the simulator.
- `make` targets live at the repo root; running one from `apps/web` fails with "No rule".

**For a skill.** `testing`: room tests on a small phone use 375 by 500 and the install banner
(added this pass). `walk-app`: on the simulator, check the drawer's size as well as whether the
verse shows.
