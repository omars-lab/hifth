# Loop pass 6: the larger-text choice goes to the owner

**What was done.** A script now photographs the three larger-text ways on the iPhone SE simulator
(`docs/design/larger-text-options/shoot.sh`). The pictures found one more fault: in the verse
drawer grown twice over, the Same roots count dropped under its icon. A browser test went red
first (52 px apart), and the icon and count are now one piece. The decision page is open for the
owner (larger-text-way), recommending follow the phone, with our own size kept as a setting.

The Nov 13 walk-capture note was folded into `testing`, `issues` and `walk-app`.

**What went wrong, and what to keep.**

- The first version of the test passed for the wrong reason: when it could not find the icon it
  returned -1, and -1 is less than 8. Assert that the setup took (both pieces found, the drawer
  really grown past 1.6 times) before asserting the result.
- A larger root font in the test browser is not the same as a large phone text size: it shrank the
  room under the verse and the drawer stayed at its usual size. Fake the phone's answer instead.
- Chromium drops `font: -apple-system-body` from an inline style, so the probe could not be found
  by its font; find it by its shape (a hidden, absolutely placed `<i>`).
- The waiting page finds a recommendation only in a heading ending "— recommended" or a line
  starting "**Recommended**"; a recommendation inside a callout is not seen.

**For a skill.** `testing`: assert the setup took, not only the result (added to "Adding a regression"). `decide`: the
