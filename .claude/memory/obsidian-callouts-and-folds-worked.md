The owner (2026-09-30) singled out two mechanisms in docs/waiting-on-you.md as the ones they
like: the `> [!tip] Recommended` callout that lifts the one line to act on, and the folded
`> [!example]- 5 more pictures from the page` that keeps secondary pictures one click away.
They asked for the tone and approach to be written up as a skill, which is now
`/note-manager:write-obsidian-note` in the oeid plugin marketplace (plugins/note-manager/skills/write-obsidian-note/: SKILL.md, rubric.md, example.md).

**Why:** short-first with the detail folded is what makes a note someone can act on, rather
than a wall of text. It is the same idea as [[short-first-guides-mermaid]], carried into Obsidian.

**How to apply:** for any note read in the docs vault, follow that skill: question headings
with counts, a single tip callout per item, folds for pictures and steps, `%% %%` stamps
instead of HTML comments, and open it by path, then screenshot it.
