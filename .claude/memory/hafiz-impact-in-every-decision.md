Owner (2026-09-26): "in our artifact, we don't capture the hafiz's perspective and impact ... which feature would the hafiz benefit from the most ... (always assuming correctness of text)".

**Why:** the decision pages argued data sources, licences and looks, but never said what a choice does for the person the app is for. Text correctness is a separate track (validation). Setting it aside for this question leaves the real one: does this help a hafiz recite without slipping?

**How to apply:**
- Every options page and decision record answers "What does this change for a hafiz?" per option. This belongs with the checklist in [[present-options-with-pros-cons-implications]].
- Rank backlog work by that benefit.
- My ranking, given to the owner:
  1. Look-alike verses, with the words that differ shown.
  2. Marking a slip where it happened, linked to its twin.
  3. What hasn't been revised lately.
  4. Getting back to your place.
  5. Comfort such as the page curl.
- Letter-level parts rank below vowel-level ones for a hafiz.
