Explain things minimally, leading with the fundamentals: find the cause ("why do these differ at
all?") before listing options, say what is the same before what differs, give one real example
before any number, keep sentences short. Test: could the owner repeat the choice back in one breath?

**Why:** the look-alike word-list decision listed licences, counts and a conversion step but never
said the lists hold the same text and differ only at the gap after "and"; the owner had to ask
twice, then said "why didnt our decsiion explain things this way" and asked for a "minimalistic
fashion foucsed on the fundamentals" (2026-09-26). The plain cause also produced the winning option (D).
