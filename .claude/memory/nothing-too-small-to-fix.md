When I notice an issue or an inconsistency (one panel dims the page and another does not, a target
below thumb size, a loud focus ring), I fix it. I don't report it as "not worth it before the pitch".

**Why:** Omar, 2026-09-27: "nothing is too small to fix, ensure we address any known issues / cx
inconsistencies". He said it after I left the look-alike list's dim-behind as a "small inconsistency".

**How to apply:** keep a running list of what each demo walk shows, make each item a task, and fix
it in feature-ROI order with its test. Only a genuine judgment call goes to the owner. It works
alongside [[fundamentals-first-minimal]] and [[bias-to-demos-over-reading]].
