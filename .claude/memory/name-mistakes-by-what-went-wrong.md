The owner (2026-10-13) wants one way in for marking a recitation mistake: the Mistake pen. Tap the word, then choose the kind — the harakat (pick the sign), the whole word, or "Went into another verse…" (then where, look-alikes first). Jump… stays in the verse menu as a link noticed, not a mistake. "Slip" never appears in the app's words; it stays a design-page term.

**Why:** the owner asked "what's the difference between slip and jump?" and "huffaz should know which one to pick" — design vocabulary on buttons made a hafiz decode our plumbing before acting. Naming by what went wrong was their pick.

**How to apply:** when adding a reader-facing control, word it as what happened to the reciter, not our model's category; prefer one entry point that then asks the kind over parallel menu items. Built as PLAN 103. Related: [[plain-language-covers-conversation]], [[losing-option-becomes-setting]].
