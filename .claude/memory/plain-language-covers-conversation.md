The owner extended the repo's plain-language tenet to cover conversation, not only options
pages and decision records. When I explain what the options are, what broke, what a choice
costs, or where something stands, use plain words a reader who has never opened the repo would
follow — say the plain thing a jargon term stands for instead of naming the term. Domain words
the owner already uses (verse, tajweed, madd, ghunnah, mus'haf) stay; our own words for our own
plumbing (salience, shard, skin, family, ETL) go.

**Why:** the app is for huffaz and its decisions get made by people not in the weeds of the
code; jargon in how I report to the owner has the same failure mode as jargon on a decision
page — it makes the owner re-derive a term before they can have an opinion.

**How to apply:** the tenet now lives at the top of the PROJECT-level CLAUDE.md
(`/Users/omareid/Workspace/git/hifth/CLAUDE.md`), NOT the global one — the owner redirected it
there twice ("we should have one at the project level"; "its that claude.md we should be writing
to"). So repo-specific working rules belong in the repo's own CLAUDE.md. Related: the [[decide]]
skill and the CLAUDE.md "Decisions are explained in plain language" tenet are the checked-in-page
half of the same rule.
