What Hifth keeps about you

The app

Hifth keeps your marks, notes and reading on your own device. The web app at hifth.app/read stores them in your browser. Nothing you mark is sent to us.

The "tell me when the apps are out" list

If you leave your email on the front page, we keep two things: the email and the date you left it. We use it for one thing: to write when the Mac and iPhone apps ship, and perhaps to invite you to try them early. We do not share it or sell it, and we do not add you to anything else.

It is stored in Cloudflare's database, on the account that runs hifth.app. Only the person who runs Hifth can read it.

Every email we send has a link that takes you off the list at once. To leave before then, write to [contact address to come].

Spam check

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It runs in your browser when you use the form, and Cloudflare's own privacy terms cover it.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
