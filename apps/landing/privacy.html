What Hifth keeps about you

The app

Hifth keeps your marks, notes and reading on your own device. The web app at hifth.app/read stores them in your browser. Nothing you mark is sent to us.

The "tell me when the apps are out" list

If you leave your email on the front page, we send one email with a button to confirm it. You are only on the list once you press it. We use the list for one thing: to write when the Mac and iPhone apps ship, and perhaps to invite you to try them early. We do not share it or sell it, and we do not add you to anything else.

For each address we keep the email, when you left it, when you confirmed it, the language you used, and the code in your leave link. An address nobody confirms is deleted after 7 days.

The list is stored in a Supabase database that only the person who runs Hifth can read. The website itself can only add, confirm or remove an address; it cannot read the list. The service that sends the emails will be named here before the list opens.

Every email we send has a link that takes you off the list at once. To leave before then, write to [contact address to come].

Spam check

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It runs in your browser when you use the form, and Cloudflare's own privacy terms cover it.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
