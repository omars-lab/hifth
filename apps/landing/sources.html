Where Hifth's pages and colours come from

Hifth is built on other people's work: the printed pages, the tajweed colours, the look-alike verses and the rest. Each one is named here with the terms its makers give, and a link to their own page. The app shows the same list in its info panel.

  • Mushaf pages of the Madinah print (digital edition) King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex · converted to SVG and adapted by quran-svg (Quranpedia) Free to use in software under the Complex's terms · this does not mean the Complex has approved this app github.com/quranpedia/quran-svg
  • Look-alike verses Quran Mutashabihat Data · Waqar Ahmed Free to use, with the source named github.com/Waqar144/Quran_Mutashabihat_Data
  • Word roots and grammar Quranic Arabic Corpus · Kais Dukes, University of Leeds GNU GPL corpus.quran.com
  • Tajweed rules quran-tajweed · Collin Fair CC BY 4.0 github.com/cpfair/quran-tajweed
  • Mushaf divisions (juz and hizb) Tanzil Project CC BY tanzil.net
  • Word positions Mushaf Database Ongoing charity (sadaqah jariyah) · free to use github.com/mushafdatabase
  • Recitation Sheikh Muhammad Siddiq al-Minshawi (Murattal) · streamed from quran.com All rights belong to their owners quran.com
