Help with Hifth

Something not working, or a question about the app? Write to hello@hifth.app. A person reads every message, and you will hear back.

Sending a problem report from the app

The quickest way to tell us about a fault is from inside the app, because the report says which version you have and what went wrong.

  1. Open the info panel and find "Something not working?". If the app stopped with an error, the same button is on that screen.
  2. Press "Report a problem". The app shows you the whole report before anything else happens.
  3. Press "Open it in your mail app". Your own mail app opens with the report in it. Add what happened if you like, then send it. Nothing is sent until you press send.

The report holds the app's version, the kind of device, the screen size, the language, the last few errors and the names of your last few steps, such as "turned to another page", never which page. It never holds your notes, your marks, your bookmarks or a list of the pages you read. The privacy page has the rest.

Problems we know about

  • In the Arabic version, a few counts use a word form that does not agree with the number in front of it. A hafiz is checking the right forms, and the fix will come in an update.

Your marks and notes

Hifth keeps your marks and notes on your own device, not on a server, so we cannot see them or bring them back for you. If you remove the app, or clear the browser's data for hifth.app, they go with it.

Something about a source

If your work is in Hifth and is not named on the sources page, or is named wrongly, write to the same address and it will be put right.
